Dead Yard: Zombie Defense · Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This policy explains what the game Dead Yard: Zombie Defense ("the game") collects, why, and how you can delete it. The game is made by Puffy Interactive ("we"). Questions: timon@phasedigital.agency.

The short version

  • Your progress is saved on your device, and on a phone a backup copy is kept online so you can get your game back on a new phone.
  • For that, the game creates an anonymous account with no name, email or phone number. On iPhone and iPad, if you're signed in to Game Center, the account is tied to your Game Center player; on Android, to your Google Play Games player if you use Play Games.
  • We don't sell your data. Ads come from Unity LevelPlay and its configured ad networks. This release requests non-personalized ads and opts out of advertising device identifiers.
  • You can delete everything online from inside the game: Pause › DELETE MY ONLINE DATA.

Your save

Your save file (your yard, progress, settings and in-game currencies) is stored on your device. On a phone, a copy of it is also kept in your account with Unity Cloud Save, a couple of minutes after it changes, so you can get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling. Only your account can read that copy.

What goes online, and why

On a phone, the game signs in to Unity Gaming Services (operated by Unity Technologies) when you open it, for the save backup, and later for the Neighbors and auction house features. We use:

DataWhy
An anonymous player ID and a generated player name with a number (e.g. SWIFTELATEDPAN#2207)So friends can find you by code. It isn't linked to your name, email or phone number.
A copy of your save, with an ID for the device it came fromSo you can get your game back on a new phone, and so the game can tell you when a newer save is on another phone. Only you can read it.
On iPhone and iPad, your Game Center player ID (if you're signed in to Game Center); on Android, your Google Play Games player ID (if you use Play Games)So your account and your save follow you to a new device.
Your "camp": the portrait you picked, your horde and home level, your weekly scores, your yard's layout and any crates you leave for friendsSo the friends you accept can see your yard, visit it and share crates. Only friends you said yes to can see it.
Your friends list and friend requestsThe Neighbors feature.
Your auction house listings and salesSo other players can buy what you list, and you get paid.

Unity processes this data for us under its own privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. We receive only a receipt confirming what was bought.

Ads

The game shows interstitial ads after hordes and at natural breaks after five minutes of active gameplay. If those triggers coincide, they share one ad. Optional rewarded ads are also available.

Ads are served by Unity LevelPlay (operated by Unity Technologies), ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. To deliver and measure ads, these services may process your IP address, device type and settings, and interactions with an ad. This release requests non-personalized ads, sends a do-not-sell signal, and opts out of advertising device identifiers. It does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. See Unity's privacy policy for the services' data practices.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, "your crew is home"). They're created on your device and don't send any data to us. You can turn them off in your device's settings.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. The online features use no names, emails or free-text chat.

Deleting your data

In the game, open the pause menu and tap DELETE MY ONLINE DATA twice (it's also on the Neighbors page). This deletes your account, save backup, camp, friends and auction data from Unity's servers and signs you out. Nothing goes online again until you tap GO ONLINE AGAIN. The game on your device stays as it is. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.

How long we keep it

Online data is kept while your account exists. Auction listings expire after 48 hours, and crates for friends after a week. If you delete your account, Unity removes its data across its services.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
